Re-Sell Assistant privacy

Re-Sell Assistant is a personal tool its owner uses to sell their own used items.

  • Items, photos and listings are stored on the owner's phone and, if they choose, in their own Google Sheet and Google Drive.
  • When the owner connects eBay, the app uses eBay's APIs only to create and update the owner's own listings and to read the owner's own business policies. eBay sign-in tokens are kept in the owner's private Google Apps Script.
  • The app does not collect, store or share personal data about other eBay users, and it does not sell or share any data with third parties.
  • Photos and item details are sent to Anthropic's Claude API only when the owner uses a Claude feature.

Questions: contact the app's owner through their eBay account.
